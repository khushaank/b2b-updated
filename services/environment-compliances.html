Pollution Control Board Approvals

Navigating the complex regulatory landscape of environmental laws is crucial for any industrial operation. We provide end-to-end liaisoning and consultancy services to obtain necessary clearances from State Pollution Control Boards (SPCB) and the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB).

Key Statutory Approvals

  • Consent to Establish (CTE): The primary permission required before starting construction or establishing any industry.
  • Consent to Operate (CTO): The final approval required before starting actual production, valid for a specific period.
  • Hazardous Waste Authorization: Permission for handling, generating, or storing hazardous waste categories.
  • Borewell Registration: CGWA NOC for groundwater abstraction as per government guidelines.
