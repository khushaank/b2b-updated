Safety is What You Do When No One is Watching

Statistics show that over 80-90% of workplace accidents are caused by "Unsafe Acts" rather than "Unsafe Conditions." Behavioral Based Safety (BBS) is a proactive approach that focuses on the human element of risk management.

B2B Industrial Solutions provides comprehensive BBS training and implementation support. We help organizations move away from "blame-culture" toward a "partnership-culture" where employees look out for one another’s safety through peer-to-peer observations.

The BBS Implementation Cycle

Observation

Trained observers watch for specific behaviors during tasks and record safe vs. at-risk actions without identifying names.

Tools

Feedback

Immediate, respectful dialogue between observer and worker to reinforce safe habits or discuss barriers to safety.

Training

Data Analysis

Aggregating observation data to identify "hotspots"—specific tasks or shifts where safety barriers are frequent.

Analytics

Reinforcement

Celebrating progress and addressing organizational barriers (like poor tools or extreme fatigue) identified by the data.
